Corporate & Public Workshops

10 Practical Business Workshops.

01. Business Health Check & Performance Improvement Workshop

Ideal for: Business owners, directors and managers

Key Topics:
  • Business performance assessment
  • Sales, gross profit and net profit analysis
  • Cash-flow and cost-structure review
  • Customer and competitor analysis
  • KPI assessment
  • Identifying critical business problems
  • 90-day improvement planning

Outcome: Participants learn a practical framework to diagnose business problems and create a prioritized improvement plan.

02. Profit Improvement & Cost Reduction Workshop

Ideal for: SME owners, finance managers, operations managers and management teams

Key Topics:
  • Gross margin and contribution analysis
  • Product and customer profitability
  • Cost leakage identification
  • Pricing and margin improvement
  • Break-even analysis
  • Waste and unnecessary-cost reduction
  • Profit improvement action planning

Outcome: A practical framework for identifying cost savings and measurable profit-improvement opportunities.

03. Manufacturing Costing, Productivity & Process Improvement Workshop

Ideal for: Factory owners, production managers, costing/finance teams and supervisors

Key Topics:
  • BOM and material costing
  • Labour and machine-hour costing
  • Factory overhead allocation
  • Standard costing and cost per unit
  • Capacity and machine utilization
  • Labour productivity
  • Scrap, rejection and wastage reduction
  • Process improvement and manufacturing KPIs

Outcome: Participants learn how to calculate realistic manufacturing costs and identify productivity and cost-reduction opportunities.

04. Sales Excellence & Customer Development Workshop

Ideal for: Sales managers, sales executives, business owners and customer-facing teams

Key Topics:
  • Sales planning and target setting
  • Customer segmentation
  • Lead generation and prospecting
  • Sales pipeline management
  • Sales conversion techniques
  • Negotiation and objection handling
  • Key-account management
  • Sales KPIs and performance reviews

Outcome: A structured sales process designed to improve customer acquisition, conversion and sales productivity.

05. Domestic Market Development & Distributor Management Workshop

Ideal for: Business owners, sales managers, distributors and channel managers

Key Topics:
  • Domestic market assessment
  • Territory and channel strategy
  • Distributor selection
  • Distributor appointment and commercial structures
  • Territory targets and sales planning
  • Distributor margins and incentives
  • Stock and secondary-sales monitoring
  • Distributor performance reviews

Outcome: Participants learn how to build, manage and improve a performance-driven domestic distribution network.

06. Export Market Development & International Sales Workshop

Ideal for: Exporters, manufacturers, business owners and international sales teams

Key Topics:
  • Export-readiness assessment
  • Target-market selection
  • International market research
  • Buyer, importer and distributor identification
  • Export pricing and positioning
  • International lead generation
  • Sales pipeline development
  • Export market-entry planning

Outcome: A practical framework for identifying international opportunities and developing a structured export sales pipeline.

07. Digital Marketing, Lead Generation & Social Media Growth Workshop

Ideal for: SME owners, marketing teams, sales teams and entrepreneurs

Key Topics:
  • Digital marketing strategy
  • Customer personas and online positioning
  • Facebook and Instagram marketing
  • LinkedIn for B2B lead generation
  • Content strategy and content planning
  • Paid advertising fundamentals
  • Website and landing-page conversion
  • Lead-generation funnels
  • Digital marketing KPIs

Outcome: Participants learn how to build a practical digital marketing system that attracts, captures and converts prospects.

08. AI for Business, Marketing & Productivity Workshop

Ideal for: Business owners, managers, sales and marketing teams

Key Topics:
  • AI fundamentals for business
  • AI-assisted market and competitor research
  • AI for content creation and marketing
  • AI for sales prospecting and customer communication
  • AI-assisted business analysis and reporting
  • Prompting techniques for business tasks
  • AI workflow and productivity applications
  • Responsible and practical use of AI

Outcome: Participants learn practical ways to use AI to improve research, marketing, sales, analysis, communication and daily productivity.

09. Business Planning, Strategy & KPI Management Workshop

Ideal for: Business owners, directors, GMs and department managers

Key Topics:
  • Vision, mission and business objectives
  • SWOT and strategic analysis
  • Market opportunities and competitive strategy
  • Annual business planning
  • Department and company KPIs
  • Performance dashboards
  • Management review meetings
  • 90-day and 12-month action planning

Outcome: Participants develop a structured approach to strategy, planning, KPI management and execution.

10. SME Financial Management, Pricing & Working Capital Workshop

Ideal for: Business owners, non-finance managers, finance teams and entrepreneurs

Key Topics:
  • Understanding financial statements
  • Gross margin and net profit
  • Product and customer profitability
  • Pricing strategy
  • Break-even and contribution analysis
  • Inventory and working capital management
  • Receivables and cash-flow improvement
  • Financial KPIs for management decisions

Outcome: Participants gain practical financial-management skills to make better pricing, cost, inventory and cash-flow decisions.
