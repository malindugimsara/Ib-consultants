Practical Business, Profitability & Growth Solutions.

IB Consultants supports business owners and management teams to improve processes, reduce costs, increase profitability, develop domestic and international markets, strengthen distribution channels and build sustainable management systems.

We do not focus only on recommendations. Our approach is designed to move from diagnosis to practical implementation, KPI measurement, and continuous improvement. We partner with Sri Lankan SMEs and manufacturing businesses to transform challenges into measurable growth.

Our Proven Approach:

  • Diagnose: Assess the current condition and identify critical issues.
  • Plan: Develop structured strategies and 90-day roadmaps.
  • Implement: Work on-the-ground to execute operational changes.
  • Measure: Track progress through strict financial and operational KPIs.
  • Improve: Establish a culture of continuous business transformation.
