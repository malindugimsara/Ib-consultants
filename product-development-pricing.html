Frequently Asked Questions

Target costing is a pricing method where we first determine the ideal selling price based on market research, and then work backward to ensure your production costs (BOM and standard costs) allow for a healthy profit margin.

We conduct a product portfolio and SKU profitability review to identify which specific items are driving your profits and which are draining resources, allowing you to optimize your product mix.

A go-to-market (GTM) plan is a comprehensive strategy that details how your new product will engage customers and gain competitive advantage. It covers pricing, sales channels, and promotional tactics.
