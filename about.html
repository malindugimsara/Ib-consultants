Founder Perspective

Making Practical Guidance Accessible to Entrepreneurs & SMEs

The idea for IB Consultants emerged during the COVID-19 pandemic, when widespread business disruption highlighted the importance of adaptability, cost awareness, customer focus, market intelligence, and disciplined decision-making. The concept was developed further and formally implemented in 2024 with the intention of making practical business guidance more accessible to entrepreneurs and SMEs.

The Consulting Approach: Understand the business, analyse its challenges, identify practical opportunities, develop a strategy, support implementation where required, and encourage continuous improvement to build businesses that are better prepared for sustainable growth.
